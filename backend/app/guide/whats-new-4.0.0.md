DisPatch is now a chat app first, with three kinds of profile and private chats.

**Profiles.** There are three kinds now. An **Admin** runs the install (settings, connections, the other profiles). A **User** makes their own bots and copies household ones. A **Basic** profile gets the bots an Admin gives it. PINs and passwords are optional everywhere. The old Restricted tier, Safe Mode, quiet hours and daily limits are gone.

**Your chats are private.** A chat belongs to the profile it was had in. Nobody else can open it in the app, Admins included. The same goes for your own bots, your File Server uploads and your Image Studio pictures.

**The DisPatch profile.** Locked and signed-out devices land on **DisPatch**, a shared profile with no PIN. Everyone who uses it shares its bots and chats, and anyone can reset it. **Switch profile** in the profile menu takes you to your own.

**Your own Atlas.** Every new Admin and User profile gets its own Atlas, who opens with a short tour of DisPatch; an existing profile gets one with **Reset to default**. **Make a bot** creates one of your own, by hand or by describing it to the AI; **Make my own copy** copies a household bot's setup, never its chats.

**Profile pictures.** Settings → Account → Profile picture: choose a picture and drag the square over your face. Your picture is visible to everyone who uses this DisPatch, including on the sign-in screen. The shared DisPatch profile's picture is set by an Admin.

**Bot pictures.** A picture for your own bot (uploaded, picked or drawn by the AI) opens the face picker: drag the square over the face and that becomes the avatar.

**Pictures, full size.** In the picture viewer, the caption and buttons now appear only while the mouse is over the picture.

**Reset to default.** Settings → Account deletes a profile's own bots and chats and starts it over with a fresh Atlas. It always tells you exactly what will go first.

**Sign-in card.** A short card now appears after you sign in. It says where your chats are kept and who can see them. Click anywhere or press × to close it.

**Built to last.** A nightly tidy-up keeps the database fast after months of use. No chat is ever deleted by it, and generated pictures are kept unless an Admin chooses otherwise (Settings → System). Files that no chat or bot uses any more go to a trash folder and are removed two weeks later.

**Voice.** With DisPatch Voice 1.6, cloned voices belong to the profile that made them.

**After this update:** reload any DisPatch tab that was already open, once.
