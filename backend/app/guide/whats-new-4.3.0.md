Safer sign-in, agents that repeat a task on a timetable, and your bots in your own order.

**Sign out and Switch profile.** The Lock button is gone. At the bottom of the bar, **Sign out to DisPatch** (or to your default profile's name) leaves your profile and opens the shared one; it is hidden while you are on the shared profile. **Switch profile**, the very last button, moves straight to another profile. Admins choose the shared profile in Settings → People & Access → **Default profile**, or pick **No default** to show the sign-in screen instead. The note about where your chats are saved now shows at most once a day on each device.

**Put your bots in order.** In Settings, open **Characters** and stay on **Mine**. Drag a bot by its grip, or open its ☰ menu and choose **Move up** or **Move down**. The bar follows the same order, and you can drag bots there too. Only your own bots move; nobody else's list changes. Basic profiles can now put their bots in order the same way.

**Keep a bot off the bar on this device.** In the same ☰ menu, **Show in Tools & more only (this device)** moves a bot into **Tools & more**, and an **In Tools & more** tag marks it. **Back to the bar (this device)** brings it back.

**More switches in Details.** Under **Abilities** a bot now has **Reactions** and **New picture each chat** switches where it supports them, and the voice picker when the voice add-on is installed.

**Owner only.** The Owner is the first Admin, the one that cannot be removed. Things that run programs or read files on the DisPatch computer are now the Owner's alone: the coding agent page, the local file viewer, shell and file tools for agents, the programs agents may run, a connection key stored on that computer, outside sign-in, and showing the backup recovery key again. Other Admins still run the household: people, characters, connections with a pasted key, backups, network and plugins. Where something is the Owner's, other Admins see an **Owner only** badge and a short reason. A household with one Admin sees no change.

**Agents on a timetable.** On the **Tasks** page, open **Schedules** and press **New schedule**. Pick an agent, say what it should do each time, and choose **Every N minutes**, **Every day at** or **Every week on**. You see the next runs before you save, and the results can go into one of your own chats. A schedule that can no longer run pauses itself and tells you why.

**Limits for agent tasks.** A task now stops at $2.00 or 60 steps, and an agent at $10.00 a day, unless the agent has limits of its own. Admins change these in Settings → AI & Connections → Agents → **Default limits for tasks**. Chats with your bots are never cut off by them. When an agent asks for your OK, its card says **Waiting for your OK** with the minutes left; nobody answering in time counts as No, and the task goes on without that step.

**Shell commands that may run without asking.** The Owner can list commands an agent may run without asking, such as `git status`. Any other command still asks first. An agent that never asks cannot use the shell until the Owner fills in that list.

**Outside sign-in.** The Owner can let people sign in with an account from the household's own sign-in service (Settings → System → **Outside sign-in**). It is off until the Owner sets it up, and it works for a profile only after **Link an outside account** under Settings → Account. Your PIN or password keeps working.

**Backups.** Encrypted backups now hold every key DisPatch keeps. If a restore cannot put a key back, Backups lists the ones to enter again. The Owner can see a generated recovery key again with **Show recovery key**, after typing the PIN or password.

**Sign-in.** Your own remembered devices still get in while someone elsewhere is guessing PINs. A new install answers only the computer it runs on until its Owner profile exists.

**Links to Settings.** An address ending in `#settings/characters` (or another section's name) opens Settings right there.

**Animal Friends.** Settings → Characters → Household now has a pack card for Animal Friends: eight gentle animal bots for small children, each with a picture and a voice. Open the card, tick the bots you want and press **Add selected**, or press **Add all**. Bots you already added are greyed out and marked Added.

**After this update:** reload any DisPatch tab that was already open, once.
