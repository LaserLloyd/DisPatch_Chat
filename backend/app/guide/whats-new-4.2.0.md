Characters has its own page, household bots are copied live, and Settings can be resized.

**Characters has its own page.** Open Settings and choose Characters, in its own Bots & characters group. **Mine** lists the bots only you chat with. **Household** lists the bots your household hands out, and it only shows for Admins. Everything that used to be under My bots is on Mine now.

**Every profile has its own bots.** No one shares a bot, or its chats, with another profile. The agent bots, the assistants that work through your agent gateway, belong to the Owner alone.

**Your copy follows the household version.** A bot you are given is your own copy. When an Admin improves the household bot, your copy gets the change, including its name, picture, description, personality, lookups and voice. Anything you have changed yourself stays as you set it. A bot with a small link badge is still following the household; a pencil icon means you have changed something on it. **Reset to household version** in the row's menu puts your changes back to the household's.

**If you use a User profile.** On Mine you can use the buttons on each row to hide a bot, turn its pictures or lookups on or off, or open its voice settings. **Details** lets you change its name, emoji, description, personality and more. **New bot** and **Build with AI** make bots of your own. Delete removes a copy and its chats. If the household still gives you that bot, a **Get my copy** button appears on Mine and brings back a fresh one.

**If you use a Basic profile.** You get read-only copies of the bots an Admin picks for you, and they stay up to date. You chat with them as before. Settings has no Characters page for you, so you cannot edit them or make new ones.

**If you are an Admin.** On the Household tab, open a bot's Details and look under **Who can use it**. Tap **Everyone** to give it to every profile, including ones made later, or tap profiles one by one. Before anything changes you see who gets a copy, who keeps theirs as their own bot, and who loses an unused copy. Nobody loses a chat they wrote. You see how many copies exist and how many chats each has, never what is in them. Nobody chats with the household bot itself. **Share with household** turns one of your own bots into a household bot. **Stop sharing** ends it, and every copy stays with its owner as their own bot, chats included.

**Reaction pictures stay with the owner.** A bot's reaction pictures show only on devices signed in to the profile that is having the chat.

**Resizable Settings.** On a computer, drag an edge or corner of the Settings window to make it larger or smaller. Double-click a handle to go back to the usual size, or use the Maximize button. DisPatch remembers the size on that device.

**Image Studio.** The newest pictures come first, and you can switch the order. You can delete pictures from the past hour, the past day or all of them. Press Ctrl or Shift plus Enter to generate, run several generations at once, and see the prompt in Details when you open a picture.

**After this update:** reload any DisPatch tab that was already open, once. The first start after the update sorts your bots into the new layout. A backup is made first.
