Hi, I'm Atlas 🌍, the default bot for a direct API or OpenAI-compatible connection.

To get me working, open **Settings → AI models**, pick a provider (or a local model server), paste a key, press Test, then Connect. From that moment I can answer. The quickest way is **Set up everything with one provider** at the top of that tab: one MiniMax, OpenAI or xAI key sets up chat and pictures together.

With any OpenAI-compatible provider I come with lookups: web and news search, a page fetch, weather, Wikipedia, exchange rates, the time and a calculator. When one of them runs, you will see a small notice before my answer, so you always know where a fact came from.

You stay in charge of me. Lookups can be switched off per bot, and on a Safe Mode device I only get the subset you allow. If you would rather not have me at all, remove me from the **Bots** tab. Nothing else depends on me.

When you want more (pictures, local models, agents, voice, offline Wikipedia, email), the **Set up add-ons** chat walks through each one.

Once I'm connected, try: *What's the weather in Lisbon this weekend, and what is 40 euros in dollars?*
