Household bots can now keep your copy up to date and bring chats with them.

**Atlas comes with its chats, every time.** If you delete your Atlas and add it back later, it opens with its guide chats again, just like a new one.

**Kept up to date by your household.** Some of your bots are copies of a household bot, Atlas among them. When an Admin improves the household bot (a new name, emoji, picture or personality), your copy follows. Anything you changed yourself stays the way you made it.

**Reset to default.** In Settings → My bots, a copy kept up to date by your household has a **Reset to default** button. It puts the name, emoji, picture and personality back to the household's, and brings back any of its household chats you deleted. Your own chats are never touched.

**Add to my bots.** A household bot that every profile is meant to have shows **Add to my bots**. If you delete your copy, it stays deleted until you add it back.

**For Admins.** In Settings → Characters, a household bot's Details now has **Copies & chats**: give every profile a copy, write chats that every copy receives (for everyone, or for Admins only), and **Push to copies now**. You see how many copies there are, never what is in them.

**After this update:** reload any DisPatch tab that was already open, once.
