## Chats

Every conversation is a chat with one bot. Press **New chat** to start one; pick the bot first in the list on the left (on a phone, the New chat tab at the bottom). Your chats are listed under each bot, newest first. **Search messages** finds anything you have written or been told, in your own chats only.

A chat's menu (Thread actions) renames, pins or archives it. Archived chats are hidden from the list, not deleted; you find them again under **Archived chats**. On a desktop, an empty chat offers quick actions such as summarise, translate or fix grammar: pick one, then type or paste your text.

## Your bots and the household's

The list on the left has two groups. **Mine** holds the bots that belong to you, starting with your own Atlas. **Household** holds the bots an Admin set up for everyone you are allowed to use.

To make a bot of your own, press **Make a bot**: fill in a name, a picture and a personality by hand, or describe the bot you want and let the AI draft it. When you choose a picture, drag the square over the face: that square becomes the bot's avatar. To start from a household bot, open it in **Settings → Characters** and choose **Make my own copy**: the copy takes its name, picture, personality and voice, never anyone's chats. A Basic profile uses the bots an Admin gives it and doesn't make its own.

## Files and pictures

Press ⚡ in the composer to attach a file or photo to your next message, or drag one onto the chat. The **File Server** is a one-way drop: upload a file there and the household's assistants can use it; it is not a shared folder you can browse.

If an Admin has switched them on for you, bots can draw pictures in a chat, and **Image Studio** (under **Tools & more**) makes pictures on their own. Your pictures, like your chats, are private to your profile.

## Voice

If voice chat is installed, the voice button in a chat lets you talk to the bot hands-free and hear its answers. **Settings → Voice** chooses how bots sound and how easily you can interrupt them.

## Profiles, PINs and locking

Each person has a profile. A PIN or password is optional; set one under **Settings → Account** if you want your chats kept behind it. **Settings → Account → Profile picture** sets your picture: everyone who uses this DisPatch sees it, including on the sign-in screen. **Lock** sends this device back to the shared **DisPatch** profile (or to the list of profiles, if an Admin chose that), and **Switch profile** on the bar moves to another one.

Everything you see in a profile, its chats, bots, pictures and files, belongs to that profile. **Reset to default** (Settings → Account) deletes them and starts the profile over with a fresh Atlas. Anyone using the shared DisPatch profile can reset it.

## Where to go next

**Settings → Appearance** has the themes. **Tools & more** holds the extra pages and apps your household has added. And you can always ask me here: I'm Atlas, and this chat goes to me.
